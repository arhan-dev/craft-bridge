Our Mission

How Market Linkage works

Built around MoSJE's goals for artisan welfare — fair pricing, direct access, dignity of the maker.

Direct Market Access

Artisans list and sell without intermediaries, reaching buyers across India from their own workshop.

Fair, Transparent Pricing

Prices are set by the artisan, not squeezed by resellers — the maker keeps the margin they earn.

AI-Assisted Cataloging

No design or copywriting skill needed — a photo becomes a complete, sellable listing in seconds.

Reduced Dependency on Middlemen

Removing layers between craft and customer means more of every rupee reaches the artisan's household.
